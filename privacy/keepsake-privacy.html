Keepsake Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Keepsake app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Chaotic Good Creations (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


Information Collection and Use

When you use the Application, it sends anonymous usage information to the services described below (analytics, crash reports and subscriptions). This may include:

  • which features you use, and when
  • how long you use the Application
  • your device model, operating system and app version
  • your device's language, region and time zone settings (these are settings, not your location)

Like any internet connection, the services the Application connects to receive your device's IP address so they can respond. TelemetryDeck and Sentry don't store it.


The Application doesn't track your location. Location is only used when you tap "Use current location" on a keepsake (see Places, below), and it is never sent to us or to our analytics, crash reporting or subscription services.


The Application doesn't ask for your name, email address or other contact details, and we don't use anything it collects to contact you or market to you. If you email us for support, we use your address only to reply.


What stays on your device

Everything you add to Keepsake, including photos, scans, voice notes, places, notes, and the text found in them, is stored on your device and in your own private iCloud account. It is not sent to us, and we cannot see or access it.

Voice notes. The Application asks for microphone access only when you choose to record a voice note. Recordings are saved to your keepsake and are never uploaded to us. To make voice notes searchable, the Application turns speech into text using Apple's speech recognition, which runs on your device. The audio does not leave it.

Places. You can search for a place, type a name, or tap "Use current location". Location access is requested only when you tap that button. Your location is read once, saved to the keepsake as the place you chose, and not sent to us. Searching for a place uses Apple Maps, which is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Text in photos and scans. The Application reads the words in your photos and scans on your device so you can search for them. Nothing is sent anywhere to do this.

Subscriptions. Premium is processed by Apple and managed with RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your purchase history, linked to an anonymous ID rather than your name or email, to confirm your subscription, unlock Premium features, and help us understand how Premium is used.

Analytics. We use TelemetryDeck to count how the Application is used, for example which features are opened. These counts are anonymous and never include the content of your keepsakes, voice notes or places.

Crash reports. If the Application crashes, it sends a crash report to Sentry, a service that helps us find and fix the problem. A report contains technical details only, such as what the app's own code was doing when it crashed, your device model, iOS version, app version, available memory and the app's language. It never includes your keepsakes, photos, scans, voice notes, transcripts, places, notes or file names.

Each report carries a random identifier, created when the app is installed, which resets if you delete the app. It isn't linked to your name, email, Apple Account or the identifiers used by our analytics or subscriptions. Sentry doesn't store your IP address, and reports don't include your location. Reports are stored in the EU (Frankfurt) and deleted automatically within 90 days.
